# Findings and recommendations

| Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Technical handles 1,282 tickets, has 104 unresolved, and the lowest average CSAT: 3.85/5. | Review unresolved technical tickets and low-rated cases to identify recurring problems and escalation delays. |
| Integrations has the longest average resolution time: 97.31 hours. | Examine lengthy integration cases for handoff delays, dependencies, and documentation gaps. |
| High-priority tickets have the highest response breach rate: 32.42%. | Review high-priority routing and introduce alerts when tickets approach their response deadline. |
| Low-priority tickets have the highest resolution breach rate: 25.83%; normal-priority tickets have the most resolution breaches: 415. | Review overdue low- and normal-priority tickets regularly while maintaining urgent-ticket coverage. |
| Billing has the highest reopening rate: 8.65%. | Review reopened billing cases and strengthen resolution checks before closing tickets. |
| Only 30.65% of tickets have CSAT ratings. | Improve feedback collection and report rating coverage alongside average CSAT. |
| Average CSAT is 3.97 for response breaches versus 3.98 within target. | Avoid claiming response breaches caused lower satisfaction; investigate other factors and collect more feedback. |

Conclusion: Prioritise technical-ticket reviews, integration resolution delays, high-priority response handling, and billing resolution quality. Track these metrics after changes to assess whether performance improves.

Project limitations: Synthetic dataset; assumed SLA targets; 143 inconsistent timing records excluded from timing analysis, with tickets retained for other metrics. Unresolved means no resolution timestamp. February 2024 includes data only through February 4. Recommendations are proposed actions, not proven business improvements.
